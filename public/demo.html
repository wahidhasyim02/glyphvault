Uji Coba Langsung <gv-icon> (5 Varian)

Gunakan atribut variant="..." untuk memilih varian: linear, line-duotone, solid, duotone, atau duotone-linear.

circle-plus linear
circle-plus line-duotone
circle-plus solid
circle-plus duotone
circle-plus duotone-linear

Contoh Kode:

<!-- Linear (Bawaan) -->
<gv-icon name="circle-plus" variant="linear" size="28"></gv-icon>

<!-- Line Duotone (2 Warna Garis Tanpa Fill) -->
<gv-icon name="circle-plus" variant="line-duotone" size="28"></gv-icon>

<!-- Solid -->
<gv-icon name="circle-plus" variant="solid" size="28"></gv-icon>

<!-- Duotone -->
<gv-icon name="circle-plus" variant="duotone" size="28"></gv-icon>

<!-- Duotone-Linear -->
<gv-icon name="circle-plus" variant="duotone-linear" size="28"></gv-icon>
